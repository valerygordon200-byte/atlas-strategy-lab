# SALES KIT — dourmouse + ATLAS

Status: v1 (draft). Two products, one honesty brand.

## 1. Positioning (the one-liners)

| Product | One-liner | Who buys |
|---|---|---|
| **dourmouse (the OS)** | "A private, local-first AI operating system — real autonomous agents with full machine access, memory that learns, and local voice. Your data never leaves your machine." | Privacy-conscious power users, developers, researchers, prosumers; anyone burned by cloud-AI data concerns |
| **ATLAS (the Lab)** | "We don't sell picks, we sell the receipts — a backtest lab that shows you what it killed and why, with the live paper ledger to prove it." | Serious retail traders sick of hype; quants who value honest validation |

**Brand rule (non-negotiable):** every claim must be true and checkable. Our graveyard is the marketing.

## 2. Target customers (who pays first)

**Tier 1 (closest, fastest):**
- Developers/technical users who already run Ollama, local LLMs, or self-hosting — they "get it" instantly and need zero onboarding
- Personal-finance/trading communities (r/algotrading, quant forums, TradingView communities) — the Lab's honesty pitch lands here

**Tier 2 (later, bigger):**
- Privacy-conscious professionals (legal, medical, finance — anyone who can't send client data to the cloud)
- Boutique asset managers / research shops — for the audited data registry (B2B line, DATA never SIGNALS)

## 3. Compliant lead sourcing (no mass scraping)

These channels produce real, warm-ish leads and stay legal:

1. **Where buyers already are (inbound):** the graveyard page, the decision-card demo, the hub. Every public asset is a funnel — link to a simple waitlist/interest form.
2. **Forums & communities (contribution first):** r/selfhosted, r/LocalLLaMA, r/algotrading, HN, quant communities. RULE: be a member who contributes before ever mentioning the product; follow each community's self-promotion rules (usually ≤10% of posts). Never mass-post.
3. **Public business directories (B2B, compliant):** official directories where companies list a public business email (e.g., industry registries, company websites' contact pages). Collect only business-contact info, only what's publicly offered for contact, and record where each came from.
4. **Content:** 1 honest post/thread per week (a graveyard post-mortem makes great content — nobody ships their dead).
5. **Referrals:** first 10 users → ask for one introduction each.

## 5. Follow-up cadence (never more than 3 touches)

1. **Day 0** — first message
2. **Day 5** — one-line nudge: "no reply = no pressure; if timing's bad, happy to check back in a month" (reply only)
3. **Day 30** — final: "closing the loop — here's the link one more time if useful" (then stop)

Every message adds value or ends politely. No guilt-trip sequences. That's both legal (CAN-SPAM) and on-brand.

## 6. Objection handling

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Is it actually good?" | Show the receipts: 991/1035 tests green, the hermetic suite, the live demo. Offer a 15-min screen share where they run it. |
| "Why local vs ChatGPT/Claude?" | Privacy (nothing leaves the machine), cost (zero API spend once models are local), and autonomy (agents with real machine access, not chat). |
| "Trading? 77% of retail lose money — why should I trust this?" | Exactly right, and that's the pitch: we show what we killed and why, we don't sell picks, and the live paper ledger is public. No return promises — ever. |
| "Who else uses it?" | Be honest: early stage, first users. Offer the referral deal and a roadmap commitment instead of fake social proof. |
| "Pricing?" | (Set when the product has a price — never invent one in sales.) |

## 7. Lead tracker (schema — build as CSV/spreadsheet)

```
id, name, company, channel, source_url, contact_email, product_interest,
first_contact_date, last_touch_date, touch_count, stage, notes, consented
stages: new → contacted → replied → demo_booked → trial → won / closed
consented: yes/no — for GDPR: was contact info publicly offered for business contact?
```

**GDPR note:** if you target EU contacts, add explicit lawful-basis (public business contact = legitimate interest, but record it; honor every opt-out immediately).

## 8. The rules of the game (the honest sales line)

1. No spam — contribution-first, ≤3 touches, unsubscribe honored instantly.
2. No fake social proof, no invented testimonials, no fabricated numbers.
3. No return promises on anything trading-related. The risk warning is part of the pitch, not buried.
4. No mass harvesting of personal data. Business contacts only, sourced transparently.
5. Everything we claim publicly is backed by something a stranger can verify (the repo, the tests, the graveyard).

That's not just the legal line — it's the product. The brand is "we sell the receipts," and the sales process has to be the same product.
